How to use · طريقة الاستخدام

Announcements

Press New announcement, write the title and text in English and/or Arabic, choose the date and press Save. It appears in the "News" section of the home page within seconds. Untick Published to hide it without deleting it. Pin keeps it at the top.

Results from Excel

  1. Press Import Excel / CSV and choose the file you used to calculate the tournament.
  2. Pick the sheet that has the final standings.
  3. Click the row with the column titles. If the titles take two rows (for example round numbers above "V.P / IMP"), set Title rows to 2.
  4. Switch to "click sets last row" and click the last team or pair.
  5. Press Use this table, check the table, correct anything by clicking the cell, give it a title and date, then Save.

Numbers are rounded to two decimals. Formulas are imported as their calculated values. Nothing is uploaded until you press Save.

Typing results by hand

Press Blank table, type the column titles in the top row and the results below. Use + Row / + Column as needed. You can also paste a block of cells copied from Excel into any cell.

Security

Sign out when you finish, especially on shared computers. The session closes by itself after 30 minutes without activity. Never share the password by e-mail or chat.
